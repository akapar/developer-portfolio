Privacy Policy for Gazelist

Effective Date: July 28, 2026

At Gazelist, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use the Gazelist application (the "App") on Windows, Android, and other supported platforms.

By using Gazelist, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information Collection and Use

Gazelist is designed with a "privacy-first" approach. We do not run any central servers, and we do not collect, store, or sell your personal data.

A. Local Storage (On-Device Data)

All the data you create within the App—including your tasks, categories, Pomodoro session logs, lists, and local settings—is stored locally on your device's internal storage. We have no access to this data.

B. Google Drive Synchronization (Optional Feature)

If you choose to use the cloud synchronization feature to sync your tasks between your devices (e.g., Windows and Android), the App will request authorization to connect to your Google Drive account.

During this process:

  • Google Account Email: The App accesses your Google email address to verify your identity and display the currently connected account in the settings panel.
  • App Data Folder Access: The App requests access to the restricted appDataFolder scope of your Google Drive. This is a secure, hidden folder dedicated solely to Gazelist. The App uploads and downloads your sync files (tasks_data.json, settings_data.json, and sessions_data.json) to/from this folder.
  • No Access to Personal Files: The App cannot see, access, modify, or delete any of your other personal files or folders in your Google Drive.
  • Direct Transmission: All communication occurs directly and securely between your device and Google's official servers via HTTPS. Your credentials and sync files are never sent to Gazelist developers or any third-party servers.

2. Windows and Android Platform Permissions

To function correctly, the App may request certain permissions:

  • Internet Access: Required to sync your data with Google Drive (if enabled) and check for application updates.
  • Local Storage Read/Write: Required to save your tasks, settings, and Pomodoro logs to your device.
  • Notifications (Android): Required to send alerts when your Pomodoro or break timers finish.

3. Third-Party Services

The App integrates with Google APIs to provide cloud backup and sync services. These third-party services have their own privacy policies governing how they handle your data:

  • Google Play Services / Google Sign-In: Google Privacy Policy

4. Data Security

We value your trust in using our App. Since all data is stored either locally on your device or in your personal Google Drive account, the security of your data depends on:

  • The security settings of your local device.
  • The security of your Google account (we strongly recommend enabling Two-Factor Authentication on your Google Account).

5. Children's Privacy

Gazelist does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The App is safe for users of all ages.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us:

  • By Email: support@kapar.org
  • Website: https://kapar.org/support.html
