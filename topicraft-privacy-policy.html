Privacy Policy for Topicraft

Effective Date: August 8, 2026 (Placeholder)

At Topicraft, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy is currently a placeholder. Once Topicraft transitions out of active development, the full detailed Privacy Policy will be posted here.

By using Topicraft, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this placeholder policy.

1. Core Privacy Philosophy

Topicraft is designed with a privacy-first approach. We aim to keep your learning curves personal and secure.

  • Local Storage: All your course roadmaps, quest progress, test logs, XP points, and heart states are stored locally on your device's internal storage.
  • No Central Servers: We do not collect, monitor, or sell your learning history.
  • Generative AI Processing: When you generate a course using the AI engine, the course prompt is processed securely. We do not link prompts back to your identity or build behavioral profiles based on your topics.

2. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We will update this policy once full details are finalized. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
