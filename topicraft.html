Topicraft

AI-Powered Gamified Micro-learning Platform

Section 01 // Vision

Purpose-Driven Micro-learning

Topicraft is designed to solve the burden of long, overwhelming learning curves. It breaks down complex topics into bite-sized, gamified study roadmaps (Quests) that you can complete during a short break, making learning effortless, engaging, and highly structured.

Byte-Sized Units

Complete single lessons in 2 to 3 minutes, fitting education seamlessly into your busy daily schedule.

Quest-Based Design

Learning is structured as an interactive quest map, maintaining the excitement of a role-playing game.

Spaced Retrieval

Leverages cognitive science concepts like Active Recall to optimize retention and build long-term memory.

Section 02 // Core Engine

AI Instant Course Generation

Type in any topic you wish to learn—whether it is a specialized technical concept, historical event, or hobby. The generative AI engine parses the scope and maps out a customized roadmap containing 3 to 6 logical chapters tailored to your query.

No more searching for tutorials or looking up curriculums. You get a personalized path built instantly for your learning depth.

Section 03 // Learning Loop

Interactive Active Recall Cards

Instead of static text documents, Topicraft relies on dynamic, interactive card formats that challenge your memory and force active recall. Lessons start with informative concept summaries, immediately followed by varied quiz tasks to consolidate your grasp of the topic.

Info Cards

Structured textual highlights detailing key theories and core facts.

Quiz Cards

Multiple choice questions testing comprehension with immediate feedback.

True / False

Rapid verification questions to establish clear rule definitions.

Match Cards

Pairing keywords and terms with definitions to build conceptual mappings.

Section 04 // Engagement

Built-in Gamification Mechanics

Learning works best when it is rewarding. Topicraft incorporates classic gamification designs to build a solid daily routine:

Hearts & Lives

Mistakes deplete hearts, encouraging you to read concepts carefully. Hearts regenerate over time or can be restored.

Experience (XP) & Streaks

Earn XP points upon completing lessons. Build daily habits by maintaining your quest completion streaks.

Level Complete Ceremomies

Reward screens summarize your accuracy, XP gains, and course progress to celebrate milestones.
