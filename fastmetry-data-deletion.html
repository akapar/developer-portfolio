Summary: AKA Labs empowers Fastmetry users with complete control over their personal health and wellness data. This page clearly describes the steps you can take to delete specific parts of your data (such as fasting sessions, meal photos, water logs, or weight entries) or delete your entire account and associated cloud records.

1. Deleting Specific Data Without Deleting Your Account

You do not need to delete your user account to remove specific records. Fastmetry offers built-in tools to selectively delete any data category:

A. Deleting Specific Fasting Sessions

  1. Open Fastmetry and navigate to the Progress tab.
  2. Select the fasting session you wish to remove from your history.
  3. Tap the Delete (Trash) icon and confirm. The record is instantly deleted from both your local device and cloud backup.

B. Deleting Meal Photos & Journal Notes

  1. Go to the Journal tab.
  2. Tap on the entry containing the meal photo, voice memo, or text note you want to erase.
  3. Tap Edit → Delete Entry (or delete the photo attachment individually).

C. Clearing Hydration & Weight Logs

  1. In the Journal or Progress tab, tap on the specific hydration or weight log entry.
  2. Select Delete to clear the entry from your metrics and trend calculations.

D. Purging Cloud Backups (Reset Cloud Data)

  1. Navigate to Profile → Backup & Sync.
  2. Tap Clear Cloud Data. This permanently removes all synced fasting, weight, water, and journal documents from Google Cloud Firestore, while preserving your login credentials.

2. Requesting Data Deletion via Email (Web / Support Request)

If you have uninstalled the app or prefer to request data deletion via our engineering team, you can submit a manual deletion request:

  • Send an email to: support@kapar.org
  • Subject line: Fastmetry Data Deletion Request
  • Required Information:
    • The email address associated with your Fastmetry / Google account.
    • A brief statement specifying what you would like deleted:
      • "Delete specific data" (specify which data, e.g. all fasting logs, meal photos, or hydration history), OR
      • "Delete all data and account permanently".

3. Types of Data Deleted vs. Retained

A. Data That Is Permanently Deleted

Upon receiving your deletion request or when deleting items within the app, the following data is permanently purged:

  • Fasting session start/end timestamps and target goal history.
  • Hydration intake records and container preferences.
  • Weight measurements and calculated trend line curves.
  • Journal entries, meal photos, and voice note recordings.
  • Associated cloud Firestore documents and backup snapshots.

B. Data That Is Retained (If Account Remains Active)

If you choose to delete specific records while keeping your account active, we retain only the bare minimum technical identifier (Firebase Authentication User ID and email address) necessary to allow you to log in in the future.

If you request complete account deletion, no personal data is retained.

4. Retention and Deletion Timelines

  • In-App Deletions: Instantaneous on your local device and synchronized immediately with the cloud database.
  • Email Requests: Verified and permanently purged from all production databases within 30 calendar days. You will receive an email confirmation once completed.
  • No Marketing Retention: AKA Labs does not retain personal health data for marketing, profiling, or analytical reselling.

5. Complete Account Deletion (Alternative)

If you wish to terminate your entire account along with all records:

  1. Open Fastmetry and navigate to Profile → Account.
  2. Tap Delete Account and confirm.
  3. Your authentication record, cloud database, and local sandboxed storage will be instantly and irrevocably erased.
