Section 04 // Metabolic Physiology

Body Insights & Science-Backed Stages

During a fast, your body undergoes profound metabolic shifts. Fastmetry illustrates what is happening inside your cells hour by hour, grounded in peer-reviewed biological research.

These physiological insights are tailored to match your specific dietary preferences (Ketogenic, Low-Carb, Mediterranean, or Balanced).

Daily Scientific Boost

"Beyond the 12th hour of fasting, depleted glycogen reserves signal the liver to accelerate lipolysis, converting stored fatty acids into ketone bodies that provide a remarkably clean fuel source for the brain."

BODY INSIGHTS PHYSIOLOGICAL FLOW
HOURS 0 - 4
Digestion & Glycogen Utilization

Nutrients from your last meal are assimilated; blood glucose peaks and begins normalizing as insulin resets.

HOURS 4 - 8
Blood Sugar Stabilization

Insulin drops to baseline levels. The gastrointestinal tract enters restorative downtime.

HOURS 8 - 12
Fat Mobilization Transition

Hepatic glycogen reserves decline. The body begins mobilizing fatty acids to meet energy demands.

HOURS 12+ (CURRENTLY ACTIVE)
Ketosis & Cellular Autophagy

Circulating ketones rise, mental clarity sharpens, and cellular housekeeping (autophagy awareness) begins.

Section 07 // Architecture & Philosophy

Offline-First Security & Botanical Aesthetics

Your health journey is deeply private. Fastmetry stores your data strictly on your local device in an encrypted database, guaranteeing that your personal routines remain under your sole ownership.

100% Offline-First

No internet connection needed. Timer controls, smart notifications, hydration logs, and journals function uninterrupted anywhere in the world.

Optional Cloud Backup

Switching phones? Optionally sync an encrypted backup via your Google account or email with seamless multi-step restore validation.

Complete Data Portability

Never get locked into a proprietary platform. Export your full fasting, hydration, and weight history anytime as standard JSON or CSV/ZIP files.

Respectful Notifications

Engineered to be gentle and unobtrusive. Smart algorithms alert you only when you approach fasting milestones, never spamming your feed.

Botanical Pastel Palette

Zero Advertising

Free from commercial ad brokers, behavioral analytics trackers, or sponsor banners. Your wellness sanctuary stays clean.
